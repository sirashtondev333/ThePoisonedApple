Here It's Halloween Everyday

When they were little, Onyx Macguire asked their mother: why do we only eat candy on Halloween? Why not cookies or cake like a birthday? When they did not receive an answer that satisfied them they asked their grandmother to make a Halloween themed cake with them, and thus a new dream was born. To this day Onyx remains the visionary behind distinctly dark delights such as the Spiced Apple Cider and Spiders Cake, Mini Pumpkin Macarons filled with an orange-cream filling, Everything But the Bag Halloween Candy Cookies, and more!

Onyx Macguire

As a child Onyx Macguire dreamt of a place they could visit every day, not just Halloween where they could combine the two things they loved the most: darkness and sweets. As they grew up this dream never died, and they spent their formative years experimenting in the kitchen, making morbid and delicious desserts with their grandma and twin sisters. Upon graduating high school they attended Auguste Escoffier School of Culinary Arts in Boulder, Colorado getting their Bachelor's in Baking and Pastry Arts. In 2010 The Poisoned Apple opened in Long Beach and has been a go to destination for both dessert enthusiast and the darkly inclined ever since.
