Here It's Halloween Everyday

When they were little, Onyx Macguire asked their mother: why do we only eat candy on Halloween? Why not cookies or cake like a birthday? When they did not receive an answer that satisfied them they asked their grandmother to make a Halloween themed cake with them, and thus a new dream was born. They spent their formative years experimenting in the kitchen, making morbidly delicious desserts with their twin sisters and grandma. To this day Onyx remains the primary visionary behind spooky sweet delights such as the Spiced Apple Cider and Spiders Cake, Mini Pumpkin Macarons with an orange-cream flavored filling, Everything But the Bag Halloween Candy Cookies, and more. In 2010 The Poisoned Apple opened in 2010 and has been a must-go destination for dessert enthusiast and darkly inclined ever since.
